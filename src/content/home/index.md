Connor is a postdoctoral research associate at Princeton University's Center for Culture, Society, and Religion, where he works on the Religion and the Public Conversation initiative. He is an ethnographer of religion, science, and technology in the United States; a teacher invested in the small liberal arts classroom; an editor for Prickly Paradigm Press and *The Geek Anthropologist*; and a science fiction writer.
