On Mars, everyone knows the story: that Mars has always been home, that a virulent pathogen once drove the first settlers into their shielded habitats, and that beyond the dust there was nothing worth remembering. Lee Weir has never questioned it — until he finds an antique book by Carl Sagan among his grandmother's belongings, and the official history starts to come apart in his hands.

*Pale Red Dot* follows Lee, his friend Kurza, and his love interest Minato as they're pursued across Mars by a figure known only as the Keeper, whose job is to keep Earth's true fate erased from memory. The novel is a story about settler colonialism and the stories we tell to survive it, about artificial intelligence and who gets to write history, and about choosing to speak a difficult truth over staying safely silent.
