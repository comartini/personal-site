By 2057, death has largely become a thing of the past. The advent of Eidolon technology allows for the summoning of a dead person's soul into a smartphone-like device. Ghosts in the machine are commonplace-they're your neighbors, colleagues, and sometimes even boyfriends. 

Jackson Jones, denizen of this deathless world, has an extremely manageable philosophy about life: never get too attached, never get hung up on the future. That system works great until the enigmatic scientist he used to hook up with shows up as an Eidolon and announces that Jackson is now their legal conservator.

Emanuel Silva is brilliant, nosy, and has absolutely no memory of Jackson. Together, they start digging into Emanuel’s death, which rapidly becomes a much bigger deal than Jackson would like. What they uncover points to a corporation with godlike ambitions and a secret so dangerous it got Emanuel murdered in the first place.

As the clues pile up across a flooded, neon-soaked New York, Jackson has to decide whether he’s really the shallow, selfish mess everyone thinks he is, or someone capable of fighting for a dead lover, a future worth having, and a relationship that didn’t come with an exit strategy.
