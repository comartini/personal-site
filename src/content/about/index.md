Connor holds a PhD in North American Religions from Columbia University. His dissertation, *The Evidence of Things Not Seen*, is an ethnographic study of the radio astronomers searching for extraterrestrial intelligence and explores how the pursuit of an almost certainly undetectable signal keeps space open for mysterious and unpredictable encounters, sustaining the possibility of enchantment rather than foreclosing upon it. The dissertation won the 2025 American Religion Dissertation Prize.

He is now a postdoctoral research associate at Princeton University's Center for Culture, Society, and Religion, splitting his time between turning the dissertation into a book manuscript and mentoring graduate students in public scholarship through the Religion and the Public Conversation initiative.

He is an assistant editor at Prickly Paradigm Press and sits on the editorial board of *The Geek Anthropologist*. A Vassar College alumnus, he remains invested in the particular kind of teaching a small liberal arts college makes possible.

Outside the academy, Connor writes science fiction. His novel *Pale Red Dot* is complete, and a second, *Ghosts of the Future*, is underway. He lives in Philadelphia.
